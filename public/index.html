🛡️ Bot Constraints & Storage Rules
  • < 2 MINS
    Max Video Duration: Videos longer than 2 minutes (120 seconds) are automatically rejected to preserve bandwidth.
  • 50 MB
    Max File Size Limit: Video downloads are aborted and rejected if they exceed 50 MB (Telegram Bot API limit).
  • VIDEOS & PHOTOS
    Videos & Photo Slideshows: Downloads watermark-free videos (< 2 mins) and all images in photo slideshows. Shop products and live streams are rejected.
  • EPHEMERAL
    Auto-Cleanup Storage: Videos are temporarily written to the project's ./downloads folder, dispatched to Telegram, and immediately deleted.

🤖 Telegram Bot Usage

1. Open your Telegram bot configured with TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN.

2. Send /start to initialize the bot.

3. Paste any TikTok video URL. The bot will validate the link, download it locally to ./downloads, send the MP4 back to you, and clean up the file immediately.

💡 Try testing with photo posts or products to see the validation in action!
